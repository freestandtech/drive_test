# Desi Log illustrations

The characters and avatars in this folder are from **Desi Log**, a free Indian illustration pack
by **Juhi Chitra** — <https://desilog.in> — licensed under
[CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/) (free for commercial use and remix,
with credit).

Credit, as the pack asks ("credit Juhi Chitra and link to this website"), is shown in the v2 demo
on the rail (under "← → keys step through") and on the closing slide:
"Illustrations: Desi Log by Juhi Chitra · desilog.in", linked to https://desilog.in.

Changes: selected characters were cropped, resized and converted to WebP; for five of them
(files ending in `j`) the trousers are recoloured to the pack's own denim. In the drawn scenes
(`src/v2/kit/people.js`) an arm that lifts is hidden and redrawn in the character's colours, and
promoters wear a drawn brand bib. Source file names and checksums: `SOURCES.json`.
